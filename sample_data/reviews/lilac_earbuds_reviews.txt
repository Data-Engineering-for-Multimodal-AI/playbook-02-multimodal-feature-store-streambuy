The lilac color is unique and the case is compact enough to fit in a coin pocket. Sound quality punches above the price — clear mids, decent bass, and no distortion at high volume. Bluetooth pairing was instant with my iPhone.

I use these for gym workouts and they stay put during burpees, box jumps, and sprints. The silicone tips create a good seal for passive noise isolation. Battery life gets me through about five hours which covers most workout sessions plus my commute.

Call quality is surprisingly good for earbuds at this price. My colleagues say I sound clear on video calls. Touch controls work reliably — no accidental pauses like my last pair. The only miss is no wireless charging on the case, but at this price I can live with that.
