The built-in straw lid is the reason I picked this bottle over every other stainless steel option. I can sip while driving or working without unscrewing anything. The straw mechanism clicks open and closed with a satisfying snap, and the seal is tight enough that it hasn't leaked in my laptop bag once.

Insulation performance matches the best in the category — hot coffee stays warm for 6+ hours and cold water stays cold well into the next day. The straw doesn't affect insulation since the lid still seals properly. I was worried the straw would be hard to clean but it disassembles into three pieces and a standard bottle brush fits through each part.

I own two of these now — one for the office and one for the gym. The 24 oz size is perfect for both settings. It's dishwasher safe on the top rack, though I usually hand-wash the straw components. One thing to note: if you fill it with carbonated drinks, the pressure can cause the straw lid to pop open. Stick to flat beverages and you'll be fine.
