The powder blue is even prettier in person. These have a soft, almost pastel quality that makes them perfect for spring outfits. The mesh upper is incredibly breathable — my feet stay cool even on warm days. Lightweight too, almost like wearing slippers.

I bought these as walking shoes for a Europe trip and they were fantastic. Cobblestones, stairs, miles of museums — they handled everything. No blisters, no hot spots. The only thing I'd note is the sole is fairly flat so if you need arch support you might want to add an insole.

Super easy to keep clean. The light color worried me but a gentle scrub with soap and water brings them back to new. They've become my most-worn sneaker this season.
