These are a statement shoe. The red is vibrant and saturated — exactly the pop of color I wanted. Build quality is solid with reinforced toe caps and a thick rubber outsole. I wear them for short runs and they handle 5K distances comfortably.

Ordered these on impulse because of the color and they didn't disappoint. The fit is snug but not tight, and the lacing system lets you adjust tension across the top of the foot. They run warm though — fine for fall and spring but I switch to something more breathable in summer.

My go-to sneaker when I want to stand out. The red hasn't faded after dozens of wears and the cushioning still feels springy. I've received more compliments on these than any other shoe I own. Worth every penny.
