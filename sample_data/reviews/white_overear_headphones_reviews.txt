The white finish on these headphones stays surprisingly clean — I expected scuff marks within a week but the matte coating resists smudges well. Sound quality is warm and detailed, with a wide soundstage that makes orchestral music and podcasts equally enjoyable. They fold flat for storage, which I appreciate.

Active noise cancellation is solid for the price range. It won't match flagship models in extremely loud environments, but on a train or in a busy cafe it cuts enough ambient noise that I can focus. Transparency mode sounds natural too — I can hold a conversation without removing them.

I use these for 4-5 hour work sessions daily and the comfort is outstanding. The protein leather ear cushions don't create a pressure point on my glasses frames, which is rare. Battery life hovers around 30 hours with ANC on. My one gripe is the microphone quality on calls — it's passable but picks up too much background noise in windy conditions.
