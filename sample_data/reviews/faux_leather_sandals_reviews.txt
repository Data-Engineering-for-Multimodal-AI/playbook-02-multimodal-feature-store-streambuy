These sandals surprised me with how comfortable they are. The faux leather is soft and doesn't cause any blisters even without breaking in. The footbed has just the right amount of arch support for walking around town. They look much more expensive than they are.

I bought these for a beach vacation and they were perfect. Held up great in sand, easy to rinse off, and dressed up enough for dinner at a casual restaurant. The buckle closure is sturdy and adjustable. They run slightly large — I'd size down if you're between sizes.

Nice alternative to real leather if you're going the cruelty-free route. The material is convincing and has held up well after two months of regular wear. Not waterproof though — the footbed takes a while to dry if they get soaked.
