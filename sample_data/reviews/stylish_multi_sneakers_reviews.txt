Love the multi-color design on these. It's bold without being garish — the different tones blend together nicely. I get asked about them constantly. They're comfortable enough for a full day of walking and the treaded sole handles uneven sidewalks well.

These are my weekend statement shoes. The color blocking gives them a retro vibe that pairs well with cuffed jeans and a plain tee. Fit is true to size with a medium width. The padding around the collar prevents heel slip during walks.

Only downside is they're harder to style than a solid color shoe — you need to keep the rest of your outfit simple or it looks too busy. But if you want a sneaker with personality, these deliver. Quality is solid for the price.
