This bottle keeps water ice-cold for a full workday — I fill it at 7 AM and it's still cold at 5 PM, even sitting in a warm office. The double-wall vacuum insulation actually works as advertised. The wide mouth makes it easy to add ice cubes and clean properly, which was a problem with my previous narrow-neck bottle.

Build quality is excellent. I've dropped this on concrete twice and it has minor dents but no leaks. The powder coating gives it enough grip that it doesn't slide around on my desk. The cap threads smoothly and seals completely — I toss it in my bag without worrying about leaks.

At 24 oz it's the right size for daily hydration without being bulky. Fits in standard cup holders and most bike bottle cages. I replaced three plastic bottles with this one and it's easily paid for itself. The only downside is weight — stainless steel is heavier than plastic, so ultralight backpackers might want to look elsewhere.
