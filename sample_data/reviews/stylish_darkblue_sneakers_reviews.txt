The dark blue color on these is rich and sophisticated — much better than the typical navy you see everywhere. They look great with both khakis and dark jeans. The knit material is flexible and conforms to the foot nicely without feeling sloppy.

I bought these for travel because I needed something comfortable for long airport walks that still looked presentable. They delivered on both counts. Lightweight enough to barely notice, supportive enough for 15K steps a day. Packed flat in my suitcase without losing shape.

Good construction quality. The stitching is tight, the sole is bonded well, and the color has held up through rain and mud. Only minor complaint is the laces are a bit short — hard to tie a double knot. Swapped them out for longer ones and now they're perfect.
