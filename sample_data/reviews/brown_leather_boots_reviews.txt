These boots are built like tanks. Full-grain leather, Goodyear welt construction, and they've already survived a week of rain without any water getting through. They took about two weeks to break in — stiff at first — but now they're the most comfortable boots I own.

I was looking for a versatile boot that works with both jeans and chinos for the office. These hit the mark. The brown leather has developed a nice patina after a few months. The rubber lug sole grips well on wet sidewalks. Sizing tip: go half size down, they stretch.

Great quality for the price point. I compared these against boots costing twice as much and honestly couldn't justify the premium. The leather is thick, the stitching is clean, and the insole has decent arch support out of the box.
