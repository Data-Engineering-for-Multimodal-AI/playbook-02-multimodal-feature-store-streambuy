These sandals hit the sweet spot between dressy and casual. The faux leather has a convincing grain pattern and the cushioned footbed makes them comfortable enough for all-day wear at a conference or weekend brunch. I've gotten compliments from people who assumed they were real leather.

Sizing runs slightly narrow — I'd recommend going up a half size if you have wider feet. The ankle strap is adjustable which helps, but the toe box could be more forgiving. That said, once broken in for a day or two, they molded to my foot shape nicely.

I bought these specifically for a trip where I needed one pair of shoes that could go from sightseeing to a nice dinner. They delivered on that promise. The sole has enough grip for cobblestone streets and enough polish for a restaurant. Packing flat is a bonus — they barely take up space in a carry-on.
