The powder blue color is stunning and the build quality matches. These headphones feel premium — the ear cushions are plush memory foam that doesn't get hot after long sessions. Noise cancellation is effective on flights and in open offices. Sound is balanced with a slight bass emphasis that works well for most genres.

Battery life is the real standout — I get close to 35 hours on a single charge with ANC on. That means I charge them once a week with daily use. The foldable design and included hard case make them travel-friendly.

I compared these against the big names at twice the price and honestly the sound difference is marginal. Where they really shine is comfort — I wear these for six-hour coding sessions and forget they're on my head. The Bluetooth multipoint lets me switch between laptop and phone seamlessly.
